🧭 What This Arrangement Demonstrates (and What It Does Not Claim)

WITNESS is a live Archil infrastructure demonstration using seeded failure scenarios. It shows that a separately constrained, read-only party can expose a specific discrepancy between an agent’s asserted account and retained workspace states. It does not establish semantic recovery, reconstruct the complete intervening event, prove a branch globally safe, perform actual branch promotion, or make the witness authoritative.

1. Seeded Scenarios on Live Infrastructure:
Executes real Archil disks, checkpoints, branches (203.51ms & 461.39ms), and read-only mounts (540.47ms) against deliberately constructed failure and control cases.
2. Endpoint Surfaces, Not Omniscience:
Two retained states let another party inspect what changed (file hashes, diffs, and boundary IDs) without claiming to reconstruct every intervening action or prove global safety.
3. Separated Authority, Not Oracle Truth:
The witness is a differently constrained participant (separate execution context, read_only=True mounts, deterministic checks, and no authority to alter the states it compares).
